Recent to earlier work

August 2026–present

Valuing Urban Ecosystems in Canada

Economic activity depends on clean air, water, a stable climate, and natural resources. Yet these contributions are often overlooked in decision-making, leading to the underprovision of public goods such as flood regulation, habitat provision, and climate regulation.

I will help inform methods used in Statistics Canada’s Census of Environment program, Canada’s implementation of the United Nations’ System of Environmental-Economic Accounting (SEEA) framework.

2023–2026

Flood Regulation Service Flow and Nutrient Cycling

The Dongting Lake basin is an important agricultural region in China and a flood-prone landscape, making ecosystem management and sustainable development pressing concerns.

I modeled upstream–downstream hydrological relationships from a beneficiary perspective to assess the cross-basin benefits of ecosystem flood regulation. I also used ecological network analysis to assess agricultural resilience in the Dongting Lake region.

2021–2023

Household Carbon Emissions in Beijing Communities

This project aims to quantify household carbon emissions in residential communities in Beijing. By creating data models, I analyze how resident lifestyles impact their carbon footprint, providing data-driven insights for urban sustainability.
